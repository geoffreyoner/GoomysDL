FAQ

Tanya jawab singkat soal batas dan cara kerja layanan. Short answers about limits and how the service works.

06 / FAQ TANYA JAWAB
Platform apa saja yang didukung?

TikTok, Instagram, X/Twitter, YouTube, Facebook, Reddit, Vimeo, Twitch, SoundCloud, dan Dailymotion.

TikTok, Instagram, X/Twitter, YouTube, Facebook, Reddit, Vimeo, Twitch, SoundCloud, and Dailymotion.

Bisa mengunduh konten privat?

Tidak. Hanya postingan publik. Konten privat, dibatasi usia, atau yang butuh login tidak bisa diambil.

No. Public posts only. Private, age-gated, or login-required content cannot be fetched.

Berapa lama file tersedia?

10 menit setelah unduhan selesai, lalu file dihapus otomatis.

Files stay available for 10 minutes after the download finishes, then they are deleted automatically.

Bagaimana file disimpan?

Terenkripsi AES-256-GCM dengan nama acak. Kunci hanya ada di memori dan hilang saat server restart.

Encrypted with AES-256-GCM under random names. Keys live in memory only and are lost when the server restarts.

Batas ukuran dan jumlah unduhan?

Maksimal 500 MB per unduhan, 2 unduhan berjalan bersamaan, dan 15 permintaan per menit per IP.

Up to 500 MB per download, 2 concurrent downloads, and 15 requests per minute per IP.
